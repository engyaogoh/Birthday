📖 Her Words

The Happy Nut of Our Family Tree

hi I'm the nut
the happy nut of this family tree
the one who never stopped smiling
never stopped moving — never
not once stopped saying yes to whatever came next
don't believe me? give me 5 minutes. I'll show you.
in my house I was the entertainment
free of charge — I'd strike a pose nobody asked for
bust out a dance move that belonged to no known style of dance
and somehow every single time
I'd get the whole family laughing
dad wiping his eyes, mum shaking her head
my sister and brother groaning and laughing at the same time
telling me how ridiculous I was
I loved that — being the reason the room got louder
bitter medicine — I didn't flinch
first cup of coffee at 7 — no sugar, no milk, straight up bitter
because a dare was never really a dare to me
it was an invitation — and I never said no to an invitation
one day I rode my bike with both hands off the handlebars
not because I wasn't scared — I was terrified
but I wanted to know what freedom felt like
and I didn't want to wait until I was old enough
so I let go — for about three glorious seconds
I was flying... then I met a parked car
my spectacles didn't survive. neither did my dignity.
but those three seconds of flying?
stayed with me a lot longer than the bruise did
in secondary school I decided I was going to be a ninja
I'd watched enough anime — ninjas were cool!
got thrown to the mat more times than I can count
not chasing belts or glory — just that feeling
a little stronger today than yesterday
a little braver than the version from last week
one step at a time — that's how I built every part of myself
a plant — it died
a goldfish — it died too
a turtle — that one lasted longer
but honestly it just sat there, becoming furniture
I just didn't know yet what loving something properly actually took
then came the dog — and something clicked
this one got all of me
the version of love I'd been trying to figure out since the plant
turns out I just needed the right heart to practice on
loving without limits isn't something you're born knowing
you fumble it — you forget to water it, literally
then if you're lucky, something comes along
that teaches you how to actually show up
for me — that was the dog ❤️
in college I drew — I animated
built entire worlds out of nothing but a pencil
and a stubborn idea that refused to stay small
my imagination was never going to shrink itself
to fit what the world called “realistic”
I wanted to make things that didn't exist yet
so I did
then life did what life does — it tested the dream
I got there — I was an artist
but there was barely a market for it
so I did something practical — accounting
it nearly hollowed me out
paid the bills — drained the joy clean out of me
one of the hardest falls I'd had
but I got back up — I always get back up
I found my way to pet grooming
the same hands that once drew imaginary worlds
the same hands that hugged every stray creature
were exactly the hands this job needed
a job I actually love — a job people actually need
do you regret the zigzags? the detours? the bruises?
here's my answer:
my life wasn't built on the right answers
it was built on the courage to keep choosing for myself
every single time
life was never about the safest path
or the answer everyone else agreed on
it was about being who I actually am
I live. I love. I explore. I have fun.
And I have no regrets.
this has been my adventure
bitter and sweet, fearless and tender
changing shape as many times as it needed to
✨ so tell me — how about you?
